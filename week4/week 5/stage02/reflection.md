## Reflection 
AI helped me notice a few things that I had missed when writing my requirements. For example, I did not clearly explain what patient information should be stored in FR-01 and I did not define the different appointment statuses. AI also pointed out that my scope said to prevent “any double bookings”, while FR-05 specifically focused on a practitioner having two appointments at the same time. It also showed me that words like “reliable”, “simple” and “predictable” in my non-functional requirements can be difficult to test. Additonally, the AI did not really invent or overreach on any new client requirements. Most of its suggestions were questions about things that needed clarification. I checked these suggestions against the client brief instead of automatically adding them.

One requirement I would change after the review is the appointment status requirement. I would clarify which appointment statuses SmartCare actually needs so the requirement is more specific and easier to test. Requirements need evidence because the system should be based on what the client actually needs. Without evidence, developers could make assumptions and add features that the client never asked for. AI can help find gaps, but its suggestions still need to be checked against the client brief.
